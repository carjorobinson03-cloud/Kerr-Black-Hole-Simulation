References

General relativity & Kerr geometry

Kerr, 1963. Gravitational Field of a Spinning Mass as an Example of Algebraically Special Metrics. Phys. Rev. Lett. 11, 237–238. doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.11.237
Kerr & Schild, 1965. Some Algebraically Degenerate Solutions of Einstein's Gravitational Field Equations. Proc. Symp. Appl. Math. 17, 199. — Kerr–Schild form; basis for the Cartesian KS chart used here.
Bardeen, Press & Teukolsky, 1972. Rotating Black Holes: Locally Nonrotating Frames, Energy Extraction, and Scalar Synchrotron Radiation. ApJ 178, 347–370. doi:10.1086/151796 — ZAMO frames; circular-geodesic E(r), L(r), Ω(r); ISCO.
Bardeen, 1973. Timelike and Null Geodesics in the Kerr Metric. In Black Holes (Les Astres Occlus), eds. DeWitt & DeWitt, pp. 215–239. — analytic shadow silhouette used as the geometry validation gate.
Misner, Thorne & Wheeler, 1973. Gravitation. W. H. Freeman.

Accretion disc model

Novikov & Thorne, 1973. Astrophysics of Black Holes. In Black Holes (Les Astres Occlus), pp. 343–450.
Page & Thorne, 1974. Disk-Accretion onto a Black Hole I. ApJ 191, 499–506. doi:10.1086/152990 — relativistic thin-disc flux F(r); zero-torque ISCO boundary condition.

Colour science

Ballesteros, 2012. New insights into black bodies. EPL 97, 34008. doi:10.1209/0295-5075/97/34008, arXiv:1201.1809 — B−V → T_eff for stellar colour.
Wyman, Sloan & Shirley, 2013. Simple Analytic Approximations to the CIE XYZ Color Matching Functions. JCGT 2(2), 1–11. jcgt.org/published/0002/02/01 — multi-lobe Gaussian fits to the CIE 1931 CMFs.
CIE, 2004. Colorimetry, 3rd ed. CIE 15:2004. — 1931 2° standard colorimetric observer.
IEC 61966-2-1:1999. Default RGB colour space — sRGB. — D65 XYZ→sRGB matrix and transfer function.

HYG Database v4.1 — D. Nash / astronexus. github — 119,625 stars; equatorial Cartesian positions, V magnitude, B−V.
